RewardPulse Privacy Policy

RewardPulse respects your privacy and follows strict Google Play Developer Program data safety standards.

1. Information We Collect

  • Google Account Profile: Name, verified email address, and profile photo via Google Sign-In for account authentication and ledger sync.
  • Rewards & Payout Data: Quiz ticket counts, survey completions, referral codes, and user-submitted payout details (UPI ID, Paytm number, Bank/IFSC for IMPS, or email for Google Play redeem codes).
  • Anti-Fraud Security Signals: A one-way SHA-256 device identifier, VPN/root integrity checks, and completion timestamps solely to prevent fake accounts and bot abuse.

2. Permissions

RewardPulse requests only essential network permissions (INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE). We never request broad media storage, contacts, SMS, or call log permissions.

3. Data Security & Retention

All wallet balances, earning logs, and withdrawal records are stored securely in Firebase Cloud Firestore under strict user-scoped access rules.
