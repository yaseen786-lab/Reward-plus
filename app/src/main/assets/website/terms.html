RewardPulse Terms of Service

By using RewardPulse, you agree to fair-use participation guidelines designed to keep rewards fast and sustainable.

1. Account Eligibility

  • One verified Google Account per genuine user/device.
  • Automated scripts, VPN masking, rooted device tampering, or self-referrals are strictly prohibited and will result in account freeze.

2. Offers, Tickets & 70/30 Revenue Split

  • Users can complete up to 30 daily offers across Mega Quiz (1,500 pts) and Normal Quiz (800 pts) tiers.
  • Each completed quiz round and built-in ad awards 1 Ticket; 30 Tickets unlock the offer reward.
  • 70% of verified partner value goes directly to the user wallet, and 30% supports platform operations.

3. Withdrawals

Withdrawals are supported via UPI Instant, Paytm, IMPS Fast Bank, NEFT Bank Transfer, and Google Play Redeem Codes subject to anti-fraud verification.
